# Credits / Créditos

This project is a fan-made native PC port of TimeSplitters 2 (Xbox, USA release) made by static recompilation. It contains no
game data: you need your own copy of the game. TimeSplitters 2 belongs to its rights holders.

- **TS2 Redux team**: the high resolution front end pictures, reticles, health and shield arcs, game mode icons and button
  pictures, and the ideas of the always show reticle, tall arcs and bigger ammunition display.
- **bekoha**: the HD textures, fonts and pictures (HD fonts, crosshairs and HUD pieces).
- **Xevengar**: the textures of the Xmas edition.

If you made something used here and are not named, or want it taken out, open an issue and it will be fixed.
